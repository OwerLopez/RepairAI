# Textile dataset inventory

Sources checked on 23 November 2026. Availability below refers to the publisher record. Garment Condition Holes was also downloaded and parsed from its pinned revision.

| Source | Access, size and annotations | Role in UCRA |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [CISUTAC / Wargön Innovation Garment Condition Holes](https://huggingface.co/datasets/wargoninnovation/Garment_condition_holes/tree/3b81cb807188690222ef2a6af9cbfd2271292a32) | CC BY 4.0. 761 image exports and 761 label files. Box and polygon records. 407 maximum-resolution views after deduplication, across 406 identified garments. The retained exports are 2160 × 3840 or 1920 × 1080. | Positive-only pilot for hole localization. Background was processed by the provider. Transfer to handheld photos remains unmeasured. |
| [CISUTAC / Wargön Innovation Garment Condition Spots](https://huggingface.co/datasets/wargoninnovation/Garment_condition_spots/tree/3483dfb0336564b626e811437e802bf56a2dca68) | CC BY 4.0. The pinned public tree contains 906 image exports and 906 YOLO label files. Image resolution and physical garment count await local validation. | Candidate for a separate spot study. A spot-only annotation cannot establish that the image lacks a hole. |
| [Clothing Dataset for Second-Hand Fashion](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13788681) | Public Zenodo release. The pinned record describes 31,638 garment items with item IDs, multiple views and condition attributes. | Useful candidate for sampling garment diversity. Item-level condition requires a fresh localization review. |
| [FabricSpotDefect](https://doi.org/10.17632/6574nhzm8x.1) | CC BY 4.0. 1,014 original RGB photos plus derived augmentations. Boxes and polygons. The accompanying [article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.dib.2024.111165) gives device and sampling details. | Spot benchmark. Split originals before generating augmentations. Keep derived files out of the initial domain-transfer test. |
| [FabriD V1](https://doi.org/10.17632/vncdwps24r.1) | CC BY 4.0. The publisher reports 2,407 fabric images, with holes, stains, needle marks, missing yarn and defect-free samples. Classification, detection and segmentation data are advertised. | Candidate external fabric-domain benchmark with negatives. Validate archive layout and sample provenance before pooling. |
| [LangFabric](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.31878400) | Public deposit of industrial fabric imagery, masks and language descriptions. The [authors’ repository](https://github.com/yoyoulllll5/LangFabric#license) describes academic and research use. | Candidate academic segmentation benchmark. Verify terms for the intended use and keep the industrial capture domain separate. |

The study credits CISUTAC project and Wargön Innovation. Acquisition verifies the source revision, expected file lengths and LFS SHA-256 where supplied. Parsed annotations are validated and polygon vertices become their enclosing rectangle. No dataset imagery is tracked in Git. See [collection and annotation protocol](../methodology/ANNOTATION.md).
