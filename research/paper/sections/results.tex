The held-out set is positive-only. Table~\ref{tab:results} shows that the forest and whole-view CNN each localized few images. The forest localized two displayed boxes at IoU $\geq 0.50$ and the whole-view CNN localized zero. The tiled variant localized seven at that threshold. At IoU $\geq 0.25$, it correctly localized 8 of 18 shown candidates with 29.5\% coverage. The precision interval for this operating point spans 21.4\% to 68.8\%.

For the forest, score-based abstention reduced the number of displayed candidates from 61 to 53 and preserved the three matches at IoU $\geq 0.25$. The corresponding whole-view CNN threshold reduced the number from 61 to 40 and preserved two matches. For the tiled CNN, it reduced the number from 61 to 18, preserving 8 of 12 matches and increasing selective precision from 19.7\% to 44.4\%. The quality heuristic accepted every clean test export.

Compared with the forest, tiling increased the localized-positive fraction by 8.2 percentage points (paired garment-bootstrap 95\% interval 0.0 to 16.4). The tiled model used more sampled pixels and a different training crop distribution. The difference cannot be attributed to resolution alone. This exploratory result merits replication on a new holdout.
