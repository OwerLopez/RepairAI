## Draft highlights

- UCRA connects a selective visual candidate to user-confirmed context and a traceable repair procedure.
- The garment-grouped pilot compares sliding-window and center-heatmap models on the same held-out cohort.
- Tiling improved the observed localization counts and abstention reduced coverage, with substantial residual error.
- The Android journal retains the evidence and outcome offline for a resumable repair.

## Data, code and ethics

The benchmark uses the public CISUTAC Garment Condition Holes dataset from Wargön Innovation, pinned at revision `3b81cb807188690222ef2a6af9cbfd2271292a32` and licensed under CC BY 4.0. Source and provenance are documented in the repository. The pilot used public garment images and a software check on an emulator. Any subsequent participant study should follow the authors' approved protocol and applicable review requirements.

The [ReparaAI repository](https://github.com/OwerLopez/RepairAI) contains dataset acquisition, training, evaluation, and app code together with aggregate research results. A submission should refer to the tested revision.
